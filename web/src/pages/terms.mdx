## Plans and Billing

| Plan | Price | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | US$0 | The desktop app and CLI, GitHub through your local `gh` CLI, and Linear through your personal API key. |
| Pro | US$15 per user per month | The hosted services listed under [Scope](#scope) for one user. |
| Team | US$199 per month for up to 5 members, plus US$8 per month for each additional member | Pro for every member of one organization. |

- **Payment.** Stripe processes all payments. Plans bill monthly in advance in US dollars until you cancel. Prices exclude taxes, which we add where the law requires.
- **Pro trial.** A new Pro subscription includes a 14-day free trial. Each customer gets one trial. A card is required to start it. The first charge happens when the trial ends unless you cancel before then. Team has no trial.
- **Cancellation.** Cancel at any time from the Subscription tab of the [dashboard](/dashboard), which opens the Stripe customer portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Paid features stay on until then.
- **Refunds.** Fees are not refundable, including for partial months, except where the law requires otherwise.
- **Failed payments.** If a payment fails, Stripe retries it and your paid features stay on while it does. If the retries fail, the subscription ends and the paid features stop.
- **Price changes.** We give at least 30 days' notice by email before a price change takes effect on an existing subscription. The new price applies from your next billing period after the notice ends. You can cancel before then.

## Organizations and Team

- The person who creates an organization becomes its owner. Owners buy Team, invite and remove members, and manage billing.
- Team bills one seat per member. Adding or removing a member changes the seat count, and Stripe prorates the difference on the next invoice. Pending invites are not billed.
- A member's Pro access through Team ends as soon as they leave or are removed. A member's own Pro subscription is separate and is unaffected.
- An organization owns the GitHub App installations attached to it. Deleting an organization detaches them.
- Deleting an account or an organization does not cancel its Stripe subscription. Cancel it in the customer portal first.

## Cloud Workspaces

- A managed cloud workspace is a virtual machine that Treq provisions for you on our infrastructure provider. You control what runs on it.
- When your paid plan ends, the workspace stops and cannot be woken. We keep its disk for 30 days, then delete it. Resubscribing within 30 days restores access.
- We apply resource limits to cloud workspaces. We tell you about a limit when you reach it.
- Back up anything you need. Cloud workspaces are not a backup service.
